Anime Breaker Companions

Companions are the official loop's combat output — what you get from Cards and what you field.

In the official Anime Breaker loop you open Cards for Companions, then fight enemies for resources. Companions sit between those two steps: they are the result of your Energy spend and the thing that carries your combat and farming.

How you get Companions

  1. Gain Energy — the loop's input. Energy
  2. Open Cards — Cards are the official acquisition route. Cards
  3. Field what you get — Companions are usable in combat immediately in the loop description.
  4. Improve the set over time — upgrades and unlocks raise your output. Upgrades

Companion vs Avatar

AspectCompanionsAvatars
Role in the loopAcquired via Cards, used to fight and farm.Listed as an unlock alongside Weapons and rare items.
How you get themOpening Cards (official).Unlocked through progression; community sources describe additional farming routes that this site has not verified.
What they changeYour combat and farming output.What you play as, which is why players treat Avatars as a collection and identity layer as well as a power layer.
ConfidenceVERIFIED (official listing)VERIFIED for the unlock; PARTIAL for the mechanics

Using Companions well

  • Cover your gaps — a batch of Cards usually leaves holes in your roster; fill roles you lack rather than stacking what you already have.
  • Feed the ones you keep — upgrades spent on a Companion you will replace are the most common waste. Upgrades
  • Match the content — farming routes and challenge fights reward different profiles; keep one farming set and one fighting set. Progression

Companion multipliers, tier lists and pull rates are not published here: no current source backs a consistent set, and 'best Companion' changes with every rebalance. The Avatars page follows the same rule.

FAQ

FAQ

Common questions about this topic.

What are Companions in Anime Breaker?

The units you get from opening Cards in the official loop, and what you field to fight enemies and earn resources.

How do you get Companions in Anime Breaker?

Open Cards. The official description puts Cards directly in front of Companions, and Energy is what you spend to open them.

What is the difference between a Companion and an Avatar in Anime Breaker?

Companions are your roster, acquired through Cards and used in combat. Avatars are an unlock in the same official loop, and function more as an identity and progression layer on top of that roster.

Which Companion is the best in Anime Breaker?

No tier list is published here. 'Best' follows the current balance patch and the content you are running, so the useful question is which role your roster is missing — see the Cards page for how to read a batch.
