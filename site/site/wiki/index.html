Anime Breaker Wiki

The reference layer of the site: every documented system, what it does in the loop, and how confident we are about it.

This hub covers the systems named in the official Anime Breaker description plus the gear and world layers players ask about most. Each page states what is VERIFIED, what is REPORTED and what is merely OBSERVED in the current client — and every page carries the date it was last checked.

Gear, economy and world layers

  • Accessories — boss-sourced equipment and the decisions around it.
  • Amulets — additional gear slot reported at bottleneck stages of progression.
  • Resources — the currencies and materials the loop consumes.
  • Worlds — how the world order is used for navigation rather than for thin subpages.

How the wiki handles unknown values

Roblox experiences rebalance in place, so a value copied from a wiki can be wrong the same week. This wiki therefore publishes mechanics and decision logic, and only prints numbers that a current source backs — the same rule the Avatars and Weapons pages follow for multipliers and stat tables.

Where a page says the data is partial, that is deliberate: the system is real and documented, while the exact numbers are held until verified. Nothing is filled in with a plausible guess.

FAQ

FAQ

Common questions about this topic.

What is the Anime Breaker wiki?

A player-run reference for the systems and gear in Anime Breaker on Roblox, with every claim labelled by how well it is sourced. Not affiliated with HF x Anime Breaker.

Why are some Anime Breaker stat tables missing?

Because the live client can change multipliers, costs and drop rates without notice. Tables appear once a current source backs the numbers.

Which system should I read first?

Energy, then Cards, then Companions — that is the order the official loop runs in.
