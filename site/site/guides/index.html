Anime Breaker Guides

Every guide answers one decision, in the order players hit it, from the official loop outward.

Guides are written as decisions rather than descriptions: spend or save, rank or upgrade, farm here or push further. Each one states its prerequisites, the step-by-step answer, the pitfalls and how sensitive it is to the next update.

FAQ

FAQ

Common questions about this topic.

Which Anime Breaker guide should I read first?

The beginner guide. Then Energy and Cards, because the first session is spent almost entirely inside that part of the loop.

Do the guides use real numbers?

Only numbers a current source backs. Costs, drop rates and multipliers that shift between updates are described as decision logic instead of frozen constants.

Is there a best-upgrades or best-weapons page?

No. Those pages go stale within a patch. The upgrades guide instead gives a prioritisation framework you can apply to whatever your account currently has.
