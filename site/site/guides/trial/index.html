Anime Breaker Trial

The Trial (often called the hallway) is a repeatable challenge loop — a measurable test of what you have built.

The Trial is covered by community sources as a hallway-style run: you push through repeated encounters and the run ends when your setup fails. That structure makes it the cleanest diagnostic in the game — the run tells you exactly which link of the loop is weakest.

How the Trial loop works

  1. Enter and push — each encounter is a checkpoint rather than a single boss fight.
  2. Note where the run ends — the failing encounter names your bottleneck. Progression
  3. Upgrade the failing link only — one change, then re-run. Upgrades
  4. Convert rewards into the next push — Trial output is meant to feed progression, not sit in a menu. Resources

What the Trial is for

  • Diagnosing builds — a run isolates what your account lacks better than open-world farming.
  • Repeatable upgrade funding — reported as a progression resource source, which is why it pairs with the upgrade guide.
  • Bridging to raids — the wave-based pressure of Raids is easier once you can survive Trial-style stacking.

FAQ

FAQ

Common questions about this topic.

What is the Trial in Anime Breaker?

A repeatable, hallway-style challenge loop reported by community coverage. You push through stacked encounters until your setup fails, which makes it a good diagnostic for what your account is missing.

How do you get rewards from the Trial?

Reported as a progression resource source: you push as far as your setup allows and convert the output into upgrades for the link that failed.

Is the Trial the same as raids in Anime Breaker?

No. Raids are named in the official loop as the challenge tier; the Trial is a separate, reported challenge loop that shares the same wave-pressure feel and pairs well with raid preparation.

Why are Trial reward numbers missing?

Because they are rebalanced and secondary sources disagree on them. This page documents the loop and the decision logic instead of freezing a value.
